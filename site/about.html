About Us

A small church with a big heart

"We believe that the door to salvation is always open — and so are the doors to our church."

We are a small Catholic church with a big heart — a community-in-formation, not yet large enough to be canonically established as a parish, serving North Texas and southern Oklahoma under the Personal Ordinariate of the Chair of Saint Peter, established by Pope Benedict XVI in 2012 to welcome Anglicans seeking full communion with the Roman Catholic Church. Ordinariate parishes and churches are fully Roman Catholic, while retaining elements of Anglican heritage, enriching the faith through reverent liturgy and historical sacred music.

All Catholics are warmly invited to join our Divine Worship liturgy and receive Holy Communion. Following the traditions of the Ordinariate, you kneel (if able) at the altar rail, where communion is administered by intinction — on the tongue.

Our members generally come from across greater Grayson County, Texas, and southern Bryan County, Oklahoma. United by our love for Christ and our steadfast commitment to the teachings of the Roman Catholic Church, we celebrate our faith with reverence, joy, and warm hospitality, forming a vibrant and diverse community.

If you are searching for a church home, we warmly invite you to visit us. Saint Michael and All Angels is a welcoming and loving family.
