About Us

An hour with Our Lord

“Could you not watch with me one hour?” — Matthew 26:40

The first invitation in Eucharistic Adoration is the Lord’s own. Beginning soon, every Saturday morning the Blessed Sacrament will be exposed on the altar of Saint Michael & All Angels while Father Randall hears confessions next door — a full hour of quiet, of presence, of simply being with Him. Bring the concern you have been carrying, the thanksgiving you have been saving, or nothing at all. He does the rest.

Saturday Mornings — 10:00 to 11:00 am

From 10:00 to 11:00 am every Saturday the monstrance will stand on the altar and the church will be hushed and open. This is not a program and not a meeting — it is an audience with the King of Kings, and the most peaceful hour of your week.

Two Halves, One Hour

All are welcome — and we encourage a large crowd for adoration. The sign-ups exist for one reason: to guarantee that at least one person is present with Our Lord at all times throughout the hour.

The halves are also for you. The hour is kept in two 30-minute halves — the first half, 10:00 – 10:30 am, and the second half, 10:30 – 11:00 am — precisely so that those signing up have time for confession too: adore one half, and receive the sacrament in the other while another adorer keeps watch. Of course, you may also take both halves and keep the whole hour for Him.

Take a Half — or the Whole Hour

Adoration sign-ups live in the Church Hub — the same place you volunteer for Mass roles and log your 16:8 prayer. Anyone can join: sign in, choose your Saturday, and take a half; the schedule fills in as adorers sign up, so you can always see who is keeping watch.

One person can take both halves and keep the whole hour for Him — or, if you only have 30 minutes, take one half and know that another adorer has the other. Either way, you'll have your half-hour for confession: come for adoration, stay for confession.

Sign Up for Adoration →

Need a Church Hub account? Call the parish office at 903-821-7511 and we will set you up — then take any open half you like.

While you keep watch — confession is available in the church office from 10:00 to 11:00 am. Arrive with your hour and leave with a clean heart: come for adoration, stay for confession.
