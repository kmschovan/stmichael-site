The Sacraments

The Sacrament of Holy Matrimony

"The matrimonial covenant, by which a man and a woman establish between themselves a partnership of the whole of life, is by its nature ordered toward the good of the spouses and the procreation and education of offspring."

Marriage between baptized persons is a true sacrament — a living sign of Christ's unfailing love for His Church. In the exchange of consent, the couple gives themselves to one another completely and irrevocably, and Christ himself strengthens and sanctifies that bond.

Grounded in the nuptial blessing of the Church's tradition, the wedding liturgy at Saint Michael's draws from the Church's rich treasury — sacred music, reverent ritual, and the solemn vows exchanged before God and His people.

— Catechism of the Catholic Church, 1601

Arranging a Wedding

Arrangements for marriage at the parish should begin at least six months in advance, allowing time for preparation, instruction, and scheduling with Father Randall. Please call the parish office at 903-821-7511 to begin.
