Log Your Prayer Time

Sign in with your scheduler account — takes less than a minute.

1 · Sign in

Use your Mass Scheduler account — the same one you use to sign up for Mass roles. Go to the prayer log →

2 · New here?

Create a free account on the scheduler with your email — then tap Prayer Log at the top. Create your account →

3 · Log & grow

Enter the minutes you prayed and what you prayed. Every entry lifts the parish thermometer — and your own running total.

Your individual entries stay private — the parish sees only the combined total and how many people are praying.
