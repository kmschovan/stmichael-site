Advent 2026

Advent 2026 begins Sunday, November 29 — the Church's new year — and runs a full 26 days, from the First Sunday of Advent to Christmas Eve. Four weeks of quiet, hopeful watching for the coming of Christ: in Bethlehem, at the end of time, and into our hearts each day.

First Sunday · Nov 29

Hope

One candle lit. "Be watchful, be ready."

Second Sunday · Dec 6

Peace

Two candles lit. Prepare the way of the Lord.

Third Sunday · Dec 13

Joy

Three candles — the rose candle of Gaudete Sunday.

Fourth Sunday · Dec 20

Love

All four burning. The King is very near.

What Is Advent?

Adventus — "the coming." Advent is the season the Church sets aside to prepare for the coming of Christ: His coming in history at Bethlehem, His coming in glory at the end of time, and His quiet daily coming into our hearts through grace. The Church dresses her altars in violet, restrains her songs to a holy longing, and asks us simply to make room.

It is a season of beginnings — and this year it is also long: 26 days rather than the usual 24, because Christmas falls late in the week. More time to watch. More time to pray.

Advent at Saint Michael's

Weekday Mass is offered Wednesdays at 6:30 pm; the Saturday Vigil at 6:00 pm and Sunday Masses at 8:00 & 10:00 am carry us through each week of the season. Confession is Saturdays from 10:00 – 11:00 am.

The 16:8 prayer initiative opens Wednesday, December 2 — the first Wednesday of Advent — with an opening gathering and blessing of the prayer candles. Bring your candle and begin the year of prayer with us: learn about 16:8.

The Solemnity of the Immaculate Conception falls on Tuesday, December 8 — Holy Day Mass at 6:30 pm.

Christmas at Saint Michael's: Midnight Mass on Friday, December 25 at 12:00 am, and Christmas Day Mass at 10:00 am. This year the Mass takes the place as usual at midnight on the 24th — plan your arrival with the family for the great Midnight celebration.

Keep the season day by day: the Daily Mass Readings page follows the Ordo with each day's readings linked to Bible Gateway, and the Order of the Mass page helps you follow along at Mass. And keep Advent at home — an Advent wreath on the table, one more candle each Sunday, exactly as our cards above light the way.
