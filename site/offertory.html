Stewardship

Regular Offertory

Contributions to the Regular Offertory are crucial in covering the expenses associated with the day-to-day operations of the parish and its programs. These expenses encompass Father's payroll, building maintenance, utilities, and daily supplies, along with supporting our sacramental preparation services and liturgical programs.

You can contribute traditionally by placing your offering in the offertory plate during Mass. Alternatively, we encourage parish members to set up a recurring gift using the Online Giving tool on this website. Through this tool, you'll have full control over the amount and frequency of your contributions.

We deeply appreciate your investment in and ongoing support of your parish. Thank you.
