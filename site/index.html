I’m New

St. Michael & All Angels

A Roman Catholic community-in-formation under the Personal Ordinariate of the Chair of Saint Peter

A small church with a big heart, serving North Texas and southern Oklahoma. We worship the Blessed Trinity in the beautiful and reverent Ordinariate form of the Roman Rite — focusing on evangelization through reverent liturgy, orthodox teaching, fellowship, and the spiritual life. We invite you to join us for prayer and worship.
