16:8

16:8 at St. Michael's — Begin With Us

We all carry burdens — family struggles, illness, grief, broken relationships. 16:8 is an invitation to bring those things to God, pray with faith, and carry our crosses with Christ.

16:8 at St. Michael's is a yearlong parish prayer initiative. Its heart is a deeper relationship with Christ through consistent prayer and Eucharistic Adoration — hopeful, reverent, and open to everyone.

"Give thanks to the Lord, call upon His name, and make His deeds known."
— 1 Chronicles 16:8

The Name

The name holds two ideas together. There are 168 hours in a week — and 1 Chronicles 16:8 calls us to give thanks to the Lord, call upon His name, and make His deeds known.

The Commitment

Participants are invited to commit to two simple practices:

One Hour Weekly

One hour of Eucharistic Adoration each week — Wednesday evenings at St. Michael's.

Wednesday Evenings

Twenty Minutes Daily

Twenty minutes of personal prayer each day — at home, in a quiet moment, wherever you are.

Every Day

168 Hours a Year

Together, that is approximately 168 hours of prayer per person over the year.

One Year

Our beginning goal is 30 commitments — about 5,040 hours of prayer offered over the year. But the deeper goal is changed lives: even one person encountering Christ more deeply makes this worthwhile.

We believe God hears our prayers. We believe miracles can and do happen. We seek healing and trust in His perfect timing and presence during our challenges.

How to Join

Begin by choosing a seven-day prayer candle — $20 each — and bring it to the opening gathering for a special blessing:

Wednesday, December 2, 2026
the first Wednesday of Advent — opening gathering & candle blessing
Wednesday evenings at St. Michael's

Call the parish office at 903-821-7511 to reserve your candle or ask any questions.

We also encourage you to purchase a second candle for someone else and personally invite them to join you. Donated candles will be available, so cost never prevents anyone from participating. And you can join at any time throughout the year.

Watching God Work

A candle poster at the parish will show the commitments being made, one flame at a time. As the year goes on, gold flame stickers will be added to a shared display — a quiet way to recognize answered prayers and blessings without anyone having to share private details.

It is a visible reminder that God is working through the time we give Him.

Weekly Reflections

Each week we will post a short Scripture reflection and prayer encouragement here — something to carry with you into your hour of Adoration and your daily prayer.

First Reflection — Advent, Week One

"Give thanks to the Lord, call upon His name; make known His deeds among the nations." 1 Chronicles 16:8

Advent begins in the dark — the days are shortest just as we light the first candle. That is the shape of the Christian life: we bring our small light to the darkness, and God does the rest. As you begin your hour of Adoration or your twenty minutes of daily prayer, start with thanksgiving. Name one thing — even one small thing — God has carried you through, and let that be the first word of your prayer.

Check back each week — new reflections will be posted here throughout the year.
