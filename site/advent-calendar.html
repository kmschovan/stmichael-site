Daily readings follow the 26-day plan from Christian Pure — every citation opens the passage at Bible Gateway (RSVCE). Daily practices are drawn from the USCCB Advent Calendar 2026 (hover or tap any date). Quotations from Pope Leo XIV retain their original attributions.

Keep it simple: a verse or two a day, read aloud — alone in the morning, or with the family at supper or bedtime with the wreath lit. If a day slips past, read it with the next day’s verse and carry on. And keep the season close at hand: the Advent 2026 page has our parish schedule, 16:8 offers a candle and a year of prayer, and Saturday adoration pairs with confession all season long.
