Sacramentals Fund

We are excited to continue the impactful legacy of our Sacramentals Fund, which was established thanks to the extraordinary generosity of our church community.

This fund ensures that our church is always equipped with the essential items that enrich our worship — from the Easter Paschal Candle to communion supplies, palms, and more. These sacred elements not only illuminate our physical space but also symbolize the light of Christ in our lives.

As we look ahead, we invite you to join us in sustaining this vital fund. Your contributions, no matter the size, help keep our spiritual journey vibrant and our worship deeply meaningful.
