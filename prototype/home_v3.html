About Us

A small church with a big heart

"We believe that the door to salvation is always open — and so are the doors to our church."

Our parish is part of the Personal Ordinariate of the Chair of Saint Peter, established by Pope Benedict XVI in 2012 to welcome Anglicans into full communion with the Roman Catholic Church. We are fully Roman Catholic while retaining elements of Anglican heritage — reverent liturgy and historical sacred music.

All Catholics are warmly invited to join our Divine Worship liturgy. Parishioners come from across greater Grayson County, Texas, and southern Bryan County, Oklahoma.

If you are searching for a church home, we warmly invite you to visit us. We hope you will find Saint Michael and All Angels to be a welcoming and loving family.
