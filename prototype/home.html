Our Mission

A small church with a big heart

We believe that the door to salvation is always open — and so are the doors to our church.

Our church is part of the Personal Ordinariate of the Chair of Saint Peter, established by Pope Benedict XVI in 2012 to welcome Anglicans seeking full communion with the Roman Catholic Church. Ordinariate parishes are fully Roman Catholic, while retaining elements of Anglican heritage — enriching the faith through reverent liturgy and historical sacred music.

Our parishioners come from across greater Grayson County, Texas, and southern Bryan County, Oklahoma. If you are searching for a church home, we warmly invite you to visit us.

The seven sacraments are the ordinary means by which God gives grace to our souls.
